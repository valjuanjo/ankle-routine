# Share-out: Oct 28, 2026

Ankle Recovery & Prevention App · Tech Exploration Lab · Juan José Valverde Ayte

Speaker notes for a 3-minute share-out. One slide per heading.

## 1. What we set out to build (v1)

Guided ankle recovery: self-triage, daily check-ins reviewed by a clinic colleague over WhatsApp, three-track routing, remote plan changes.

## 2. What broke

- **Self-triage.** Personalized warning-sign routing is the hardest part to get right and to copy safely. A wrong "keep exercising" is a missed serious injury.
- **Remote monitoring.** Once a physio reviews check-ins and replies, the app is telehealth, whatever we call it. In Peru that needs a registered facility (RENIPRESS); in the US it pulls us toward FDA and licensure questions.
- **Key insight:** a label doesn't decide whether something is telehealth; what the physio does with the data does.

## 3. What changed (v2)

A **routine tool with patient-controlled sharing**:

- The physio sets the routine **in person** (QR code). Changes happen only at visits.
- The patient schedules, logs and tracks their exercises. Data stays on their phone.
- The patient **chooses** to share a PDF summary with whoever they want. Nobody monitors it.
- A **fixed** safety checklist, the same for everyone. The app never alerts a clinician.

## 4. What we give up, and our edge now

- Give up: personalized triage, fast remote adjustment.
- Edge: ankle-specific programs, Peruvian Spanish + English, price, simple QR setup and sharing, coach/team mode.
