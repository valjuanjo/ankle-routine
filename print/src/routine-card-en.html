Exercises

Ankle alphabetSit with your leg out and your foot off the floor. Trace the letters A to Z in the air with your big toe, moving only at the ankle.
Ankle pumpsLying down or sitting with your leg up, point your toes away, then pull them toward you. Move slowly and smoothly.
Knee to wallStand facing a wall with your foot a few centimeters away. Keep your heel down and bend your knee toward the wall. Move your foot back a little as it gets easier.
Calf stretchHands on a wall, one foot back with the knee straight and heel down. Lean forward until you feel a gentle stretch in the calf. Hold.
Band: foot outSit with a resistance band around your forefoot, anchored to the inside. Turn the sole of your foot outward against the band, then return slowly.
Band: foot inSit with a resistance band around your forefoot, anchored to the outside. Turn the sole of your foot inward against the band, then return slowly.
Band: toes upSit with a band around the top of your foot, anchored in front of you. Pull your toes toward you against the band, then return slowly.
Band: toes downSit with your leg out and a band around the ball of your foot, holding the ends. Push your toes away against the band, then return slowly.
Heel raises, both legsStand holding a wall or chair. Rise onto the balls of both feet, pause, and lower slowly.
Heel raises, one legStand on one leg, lightly holding a wall. Rise onto the ball of your foot, pause, and lower slowly over 3 seconds.
Single-leg balanceStand on one leg near a wall or counter, knee slightly bent. Hold your balance. Touch the wall only if you need to.
Single-leg balance, eyes closedSame as single-leg balance, next to a wall, with your eyes closed. Open your eyes or touch the wall whenever you need to.
Single-leg reachStand on one leg. With the other foot, reach forward, to the side and behind, lightly tapping the floor each time, then return to the center.
Small hops, both legsHop gently in place on both feet, landing softly on the balls of your feet with knees slightly bent.
Side-to-side hopsHop side to side over a line on both feet, landing softly. Progress to one leg when your physio or coach says it's fine.
Hop and holdHop forward from one leg and land on the same leg. Hold the landing still for 2 seconds, knee slightly bent over your toes.

This app is not a diagnosis or treatment, and no one monitors what you log. If your ankle gets worse, contact your physio or go to urgent care.
