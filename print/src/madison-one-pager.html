Team ankle warm-up: 3-week pilot

Tech Exploration Lab · Juan José Valverde Ayte, physiotherapist

What it is

A warm-up and prevention routine for your team. About 15 minutes, 2–3 times a week, before practice, built from ankle mobility, strength, balance and landing drills (FIFA 11+ style). Printed card with QR links to short videos; a phone app comes later.

What the trainer gets

  • A ready-made warm-up, adjusted with you to your sport and season.
  • A simple weekly count of sessions done, as a tool for you.

What it isn't

  • Not a medical service. No one monitors athletes through it; injuries go through you as usual.
  • No individual or health data. I receive team totals only: sessions done per team per week.
