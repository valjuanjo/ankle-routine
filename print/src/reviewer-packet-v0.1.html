1. Safety checklist (EN and ES written separately)

Stop and get checked if any of these apply

  • You can't put weight on your foot or take 4 steps
  • Your ankle or foot looks deformed or out of place
  • Your foot is numb, tingling, cold or pale
  • You felt or heard a "pop" at the back of your heel, or can't push off your toes
  • Your calf is painful, swollen, warm or red
  • Pain or swelling is getting worse instead of better
Don't do the exercises. Contact your physio or go to urgent care.
Emergency 911

Detente y hazte revisar si te pasa algo de esto

  • No puedes apoyar el pie o no logras dar 4 pasos
  • El tobillo o el pie se ve deformado o fuera de lugar
  • Sientes el pie dormido, con hormigueo, frío o pálido
  • Sentiste o escuchaste un "chasquido" detrás del talón, o no puedes empujar con la punta del pie
  • La pantorrilla te duele, está hinchada, caliente o roja
  • El dolor o la hinchazón empeoran en vez de mejorar
No hagas los ejercicios. Contacta a tu fisioterapeuta o acude a emergencias.
SAMU (ambulancia) 106 · Bomberos 116

3. Routine templates

Early stage (7 days/week)

  • Ankle pumps: 2 × 20
  • Ankle alphabet: 1 × 1 per side

Mobility (4 days/week)

  • Ankle alphabet: 1 × 1 per side
  • Knee to wall: 2 × 10 per side
  • Calf stretch: 3 × 30 s per side
  • Heel raises, both legs: 2 × 10

Strength (3 days/week)

  • Band: foot out: 3 × 12 per side
  • Band: foot in: 3 × 12 per side
  • Band: toes up: 3 × 12 per side
  • Band: toes down: 3 × 12 per side
  • Heel raises, both legs: 3 × 15

Balance (5 days/week)

  • Single-leg balance: 3 × 30 s per side
  • Single-leg reach: 2 × 6 per side
  • Heel raises, one leg: 3 × 10 per side

Back to activity (3 days/week)

  • Single-leg balance, eyes closed: 3 × 20 s per side
  • Heel raises, one leg: 3 × 15 per side
  • Small hops, both legs: 3 × 15
  • Side-to-side hops: 3 × 10
  • Hop and hold: 2 × 6 per side

4. General programs

6-week ankle prevention program

For people with a 'weak' ankle or past sprains who can walk, jog and hop without pain.

Weeks 1–2 (3 days/week). Move on when: You've done most sessions and can balance 30 seconds on each leg without pain.

  • Knee to wall: 2 × 10 per side
  • Heel raises, both legs: 3 × 15
  • Band: foot out: 3 × 12 per side
  • Single-leg balance: 3 × 30 s per side

Weeks 3–4 (3 days/week). Move on when: You can do 12 single-leg heel raises on each side and balance 20 seconds with eyes closed, without pain.

  • Heel raises, one leg: 3 × 12 per side
  • Band: foot out: 3 × 15 per side
  • Single-leg balance, eyes closed: 3 × 20 s per side
  • Single-leg reach: 2 × 6 per side
  • Small hops, both legs: 3 × 15

Weeks 5–6 (3 days/week). Move on when: Keep 2 sessions a week, or switch to the team warm-up before sport.

  • Heel raises, one leg: 3 × 15 per side
  • Single-leg balance, eyes closed: 3 × 30 s per side
  • Side-to-side hops: 3 × 10
  • Hop and hold: 2 × 6 per side

General program after a mild ankle sprain

Only after a health professional has seen your ankle and said a general program is fine for you.

Stage 1: Move (7 days/week). Move on when: You can walk normally without limping or pain.

  • Ankle pumps: 2 × 20
  • Ankle alphabet: 1 × 1 per side
  • Calf stretch: 3 × 30 s per side

Stage 2: Strength (3 days/week). Move on when: You can balance 30 seconds on one leg without pain.

  • Knee to wall: 2 × 10 per side
  • Band: foot out: 3 × 12 per side
  • Band: foot in: 3 × 12 per side
  • Heel raises, both legs: 3 × 15
  • Single-leg balance: 3 × 30 s per side

Stage 3: Balance (3 days/week). Move on when: You can do 10 single-leg heel raises and hop gently on both feet without pain.

  • Heel raises, one leg: 3 × 10 per side
  • Single-leg balance, eyes closed: 3 × 20 s per side
  • Single-leg reach: 2 × 6 per side
  • Band: foot out: 3 × 15 per side

Stage 4: Back to activity (3 days/week). Move on when: Before going back to sport, check with your physio, athletic trainer or coach.

  • Heel raises, one leg: 3 × 15 per side
  • Small hops, both legs: 3 × 15
  • Side-to-side hops: 3 × 10
  • Hop and hold: 2 × 6 per side

5. Team warm-up (15 min)

1. Jog (4 min)

  • Jog with skips and shuffles: 4 min

2. Mobility (2 min)

  • Knee to wall: 1 × 10 per side
  • Calf stretch: 20 s per side

3. Strength (3 min)

  • Heel raises, both legs: 2 × 15
  • Heel raises, one leg: 1 × 10 per side

4. Balance (3 min)

  • Single-leg balance: 2 × 30 s per side
  • Single-leg reach: 1 × 6 per side

5. Hops and landings (3 min)

  • Small hops, both legs: 1 × 15
  • Side-to-side hops: 2 × 10
  • Hop and hold: 1 × 5 per side
