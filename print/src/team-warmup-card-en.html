Team ankle warm-up · 15 min

A warm-up and prevention routine for your team. 2–3 times a week, before practice.

1. Jog (4 min)
Jog with skips and shuffles4 minJog the length of the field or room and back: straight, then skipping, then side shuffles each way, then backward.
2. Mobility (2 min)
Knee to wall1 × 10 per sideStand facing a wall with your foot a few centimeters away. Keep your heel down and bend your knee toward the wall. Move your foot back a little as it gets easier.
Calf stretch20 s per sideHands on a wall, one foot back with the knee straight and heel down. Lean forward until you feel a gentle stretch in the calf. Hold.
3. Strength (3 min)
Heel raises, both legs2 × 15Stand holding a wall or chair. Rise onto the balls of both feet, pause, and lower slowly.
Heel raises, one leg1 × 10 per sideStand on one leg, lightly holding a wall. Rise onto the ball of your foot, pause, and lower slowly over 3 seconds.
4. Balance (3 min)
Single-leg balance2 × 30 s per sideStand on one leg near a wall or counter, knee slightly bent. Hold your balance. Touch the wall only if you need to.
Single-leg reach1 × 6 per sideStand on one leg. With the other foot, reach forward, to the side and behind, lightly tapping the floor each time, then return to the center.
5. Hops and landings (3 min)
Small hops, both legs1 × 15Hop gently in place on both feet, landing softly on the balls of your feet with knees slightly bent.
Side-to-side hops2 × 10Hop side to side over a line on both feet, landing softly. Progress to one leg when your physio or coach says it's fine.
Hop and hold1 × 5 per sideHop forward from one leg and land on the same leg. Hold the landing still for 2 seconds, knee slightly bent over your toes.

Stop and get checked if any of these apply

  • You can't put weight on your foot or take 4 steps
  • Your ankle or foot looks deformed or out of place
  • Your foot is numb, tingling, cold or pale
  • You felt or heard a "pop" at the back of your heel, or can't push off your toes
  • Your calf is painful, swollen, warm or red
  • Pain or swelling is getting worse instead of better
Don't do the exercises. Contact your physio or go to urgent care.
